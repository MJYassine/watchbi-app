01 — Orientation

What the dashboard is

WatchBI is an analytics layer over a dealer's own inventory and sales — aging and quality grades, sell-through velocity, a configurable per-sale performance grade, restock (buy) signals with funding scenarios, and financial liabilities. It answers a single question in four tabs: what do I hold, how fast does it move, how well are we selling it, and what do I owe?

It ships as its own subscription website on a WatchOps subdomain, open to any dealer. Inventory and sales come from the WatchOps API, or from an uploaded spreadsheet for non-WatchOps books. This document folds the July baseline together with the God's Eye meeting notes — nothing here replaces the working requirements sheet; it states the current agreed picture in plain language.

02 — This round

What changed

Five decisions came out of the last meeting. Everything else in the baseline stands.

New

God's Eye table

A per-Sales-Associate monthly scorecard on the Sales tab — every money and unit figure shown in two bases, with an Average and a Total row.

New

Closed vs Cashed

Every metric is read two ways. Cashed is a single WatchOps status — "Sold & Cashed Out" — so no derivation is needed.

Confirmed

Budgets & pacing

A team goal splits to each associate and can be overridden. Pacing is a number the manager enters, not one the system extrapolates.

Out of scope

Lock-invoice button

Locking a cashed-out invoice is a WatchOps platform feature, not part of God's Eye or this dashboard.

Deferred

GP-over-time grading

The "A-level inventory" idea (money ÷ days held) is tabled — it belongs to a future Suggested Buy dashboard, not v1.

03 — The two bases

Closed & Cashed

A watch moves through WatchOps statuses on its way out the door. A deal is Closed the moment it's booked, and stays Closed until money is fully in — at which point WatchOps flips it to Sold & Cashed Out and it becomes Cashed. Every God's Eye figure is shown in both bases so a manager sees committed business and collected business side by side.

The cashing rule

Nothing reaches Cashed unless it is fully paid, has a tracking number, and has passed through Shipped waiting on payment. That gate lives inside WatchOps — the dashboard simply reads the resulting status.

Which month a deal counts in

A deal counts in the Closed basis in the month it closed, and in the Cashed basis in the month it becomes fully cashed — which can be a later month. A deal is never in both bases at once.

05 — Targets

Budgets & scoreboard

The budget columns in God's Eye come from a simple monthly setup, run by an owner or manager about a week before the month starts.

1

Set the team goal

A manager enters one team-wide GP goal and one units goal for the coming month.

2

Split by track record

The goal divides across associates by each one's share of sales over the trailing three months.

3

Override by hand

Any individual's number can be adjusted directly — the split is a starting point, not a lock.

4

Track attainment

A monthly scoreboard shows each associate's actual vs goal, with over/under, in revenue and units.

The exact scoreboard column headers are still to come from DJ (see open decisions).

07 — Full catalog

Every requirement

The complete set, grouped by area. Priority reads as Must first build · Should fast-follow · Could backlog · Open decision needed.

Platform & access

7
PLAT-01Must

Open to any dealer, subscription-gated. Not limited to existing WatchOps customers; without an active subscription it can't be used.

PLAT-02Must

Its own WatchOps subdomain — a sibling to Deal Radar, e.g. watchbi.watchops.com.

PLAT-03Open

Authentication undecided — WatchOps SSO or own accounts; shared with Deal Radar.

PLAT-04Must

Four roles — Owner, Manager, Buyer, Seller — one role set shared with Deal Radar.

PLAT-05Must

Saved settings isolated per dealer — grading config, filters, and exclusions live in the database, private to each account.

PLAT-06Must

Everyone sees all analytics — cost, margins, liabilities, every associate's numbers. Roles gate configuration only, never visibility.

PLAT-07Must

No native app. A responsive web app, installable to the home screen (PWA).

Data sources

6
DATA-01Must

Live inventory from WatchOps — cost, prices, condition, status, type, supplier, serial, payment, purchase date — or an uploaded sheet. Sold & voided items excluded.

DATA-02Must

Sales history from WatchOps — price, cost, condition, salesperson, dates — or an uploaded sheet.

DATA-03Must

Room for future market APIs. None required today; no view depends on outside market data.

DATA-04Must

Correct code legends. WatchOps numeric codes for condition, status, and box/papers translated via the verified legends (an earlier build had condition reversed).

DATA-05Must

Excel/CSV upload with column mapping — the importer detects columns and lets the user confirm the mapping, so any export format works.

DATA-06Must

Reference normalization. PAM01213 = PAM1213 = "PAM 1213"; variants roll up to one base model everywhere.

Inventory analytics

7
INV-01Must

Header KPIs — items in stock, capital tied up, count aged 91+ days, median days to sell, overall health.

INV-02Must

Breakdowns — by brand, brand × condition, inventory type, price tier, product line, and model.

INV-03Must

Aging & A–F quality grade by days in stock — A fresh, F heavily aged.

INV-04Must

Sell-through velocity — weekly rate and weeks-of-stock per model and brand; sparse windows must not overstate demand.

INV-05Should

Supplier spend view — spend, units, and aging by supplier.

INV-06Should

Data-quality flags — records missing key fields (no brand/name, no cost).

INV-07Should

Drill-down from brand → line → model.

Sales analytics & God's Eye

9
SALES-01Must

Header KPIs — revenue, units, total profit, median margin.

SALES-02Must

Per-salesperson performance — profit, units, velocity, with drill-in to their sales.

SALES-03Must

Per-sale performance grade (A–D) from margin and days-on-hand — own figures only, no market data.

SALES-04Should

Sales by brand & condition — units, revenue, profit, margin.

SALES-05Must

God's Eye table — one monthly row per Sales Associate, plus Average and Total, every figure in Closed and Cashed.

SALES-06Must

Closed vs Cashed defined. Cashed = "Sold & Cashed Out"; Closed = booked but not yet cashed. Month-attributed; never both at once.

SALES-07Must

Core figures — GP, Units, Revenue, each in both bases.

Budgets, pacing, over/under. Budget + pacing + O/U for GP and Units; Revenue gets pacing only. Pacing is manager-entered.

SALES-09Must

Per-deal quality — GP/unit, average aging, margin, average price, each in both bases.

Grading, UI & foundations

12
GRADE-01Should

Tunable sale grade — admin sets margin-vs-speed weighting, thresholds, and harshness; applies business-wide.

GRADE-02Should

Tunable aging grade — admin sets the day thresholds for A–F.

UI-01Must

Four tabs — Inventory, Sales, Buy Signals, Liabilities. Live alerts live in Deal Radar, not here.

UI-02Should

Global filters — brand, status, period across the analytic views. Liabilities always show full exposure.

UI-03Should

Drill-down throughout — brand → model, salesperson → sales, model → items.

UI-04Should

Exclude & restore within an analysis; ad-hoc tweaks are personal, saved config is admin-tier.

UI-05Must

Always live — auto-refresh on a schedule and re-pull on page reload.

NFR-01Must

Strict per-business isolation of data and derived analytics.

NFR-02Should

Responsive at full scale — caching / incremental compute over a full book.

NFR-03Must

Correct ingestion — page all records, exclude sold/voided, map inventory types.

CUST-01Should

Customer view from sold history — margin, dollars, buying velocity per customer.

CUST-02Should

Supplier view from sold history — margin, dollars, velocity per supplier.

08 — Later

Deferred & backlog

Suggested Buy — GP-over-time grading

The "A-level inventory" idea: grade stock by GP per day held, weighted by current inventory mix and recent trend, so a model that sold well historically but has gone quiet pulls its own rebuy signal down. Its own future dashboard — not v1. Grading itself is tabled.

Billing granularity

Per-business vs per-seat unlock — affects only the entitlement check.

Market data, if WatchOps adds it

Market-comparison views could return if WatchOps later exposes pricing.

Sales trends over time

A time-series sales view existed in an early build; reconsider if wanted.

Live non-WatchOps connector

Upload already works; an auto-refreshing Google Sheet / API connector is open.

Export / reporting

CSV / PDF export or scheduled reports — not in the current build.
